Amounts display to two decimals; checks use full precision. “Fail” is a warning, not a stopped month. Cash coverage = cash distributions ÷ that month’s interest, excluding reinvested distributions.

Timing and assumptions
  1. Grow opening investments, pay and bills by their respective annual rate divided by 12, including month one.
  2. Calculate distributions on price-grown opening investments, before new pay and reinvested distributions. Invest all pay and finance all bills.
  3. Charge a full month of interest on opening debt plus the current bill draw. Split distributions between reinvested distributions and cash. Cash pays interest, then principal; only uncovered interest increases debt.
  4. Keep any cash left after debt clears as generated surplus. Check ending debt ÷ investments and cash distributions ÷ full period interest. Neither warning changes the path.

Zero interest produces N/A coverage. Zero investments with positive debt produces infinite DTA and Fail; zero investments and zero debt produces N/A. A threshold equality passes. Negative net equity can continue in this hypothetical planner.

Monthly reconciliation: net-equity change = pay − bills + price gain + total distributions − interest. Taxes, fees, daily billing, lender eligibility, collateral requirements and forced liquidation are excluded. Distribution yield is not proof of sustainable income or economic profit.
