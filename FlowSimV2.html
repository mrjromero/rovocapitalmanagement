Cash coverage = cash distributions ÷ that month’s interest, excluding the immediate reinvested allocation. Cash coverage uses cash available before the principal/target sweep; remaining cash may later be reinvested.

Timing and assumptions
  1. Grow opening investments, pay and bills by their respective annual rate divided by 12, including month one.
  2. Calculate distributions on price-grown opening investments, before new pay and reinvested distributions. In full-financing mode, invest all pay and finance all bills. In Maximum mode, pay unfinanced bills from pay and invest only the remainder.
  3. Charge a full month of interest on opening debt plus the current bill draw. Split distributions between immediate reinvestment and cash. Cash pays interest, then principal; only uncovered interest increases debt.
  4. Principal sweep keeps cash left after debt clears as generated surplus. Target sweep repays only the principal needed toward the selected ending DTA, accounting for reinvestment of remaining cash in the denominator; if cash is insufficient, it pays as much principal as possible. Check ending debt ÷ investments and cash distributions ÷ full period interest. Full-financing warnings do not change the path. Maximum mode selects the largest feasible cent amount (also considering the exact bill ceiling), and stops before an infeasible month. It checks every piecewise-linear region, not a coarse search. No cash reserve or asset sale bridges a funding gap.

Zero interest produces N/A coverage. Zero investments with positive debt produces infinite DTA and Fail; zero investments and zero debt produces N/A. A threshold equality passes. Negative net equity can continue in this hypothetical planner.

Margin interest is approximated as APR/12 on opening debt plus the financed bill draw. Principal repayment is voluntary, not a scheduled amortizing installment. Actual M1 daily accrual, billing dates and permission to capitalize unpaid interest are not modeled. Ending checks do not protect against an intramonth or post-shock peak breach.
Monthly reconciliation: net-equity change = pay − bills + price gain − shock loss + total distributions − interest. Taxes, fees, daily billing, lender eligibility, collateral requirements and forced liquidation are excluded. Distribution yield is not proof of sustainable income or economic profit.
