How the model works

Each month: grow investments, route pay and bills for the active phase, calculate distributions, charge interest, sweep cash to interest then principal, and reinvest remaining cash. Annual rates are divided by 12. Pay and expense growth begin in month 2.

Freedom takes precedence over deleveraging; phase start months are inclusive. Zero disables a phase. Guardrails flag outcomes after each transition without changing borrowing.

Coverage measures cash distributions ÷ charged margin interest, not household debt-service coverage. Zero interest means N/A. Zero investments means DTA is undefined.

This hypothetical model omits taxes, fees, changing rates, market volatility and lender rules. It is not a broker-specific interest calculation. Passing thresholds establishes neither lender approval nor financial independence.
