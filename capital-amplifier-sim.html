Simulation Summary & Chart Guide

Understanding the Data Curves

The interactive charting component maps out your asset structure over time. Here is what each line measures:

  • Total Investments (Solid Blue Area): The total market value of all assets working for you. It grows larger than your net worth based on how much borrowed loan money you introduce to the balance sheet.
  • Your Net Worth (Solid Green Line): Your real personal share of the wealth. It equals total investments minus what you still owe on the loan. All surplus savings and investment payouts add directly to this curve.
  • Traditional Strategy - No Borrowing (Dashed Amber Line): Your safety comparison run. This shows exactly how much money you would have if you avoided borrowing entirely and just invested your own cash normally over time.
  • Inflation Break-Even Line (Solid Magenta Line): The purchasing power baseline. This tracks how much money your cash savings must grow *just to buy the same amount of goods* over time. If your green line is above this, you are building real wealth.
  • Broker Forced-Sale Danger Line (Dotted Red Line): Your critical safety floor. If the top of the blue investment area ever drops down to touch this line, your account has run out of safety cushion. The broker will immediately step in and force a liquidation sale to pay back the loan.
