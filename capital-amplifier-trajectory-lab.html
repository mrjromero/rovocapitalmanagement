Model Contract
Equity is the terminal invariant. The model grows equity until it can support the requested terminal AUM at the terminal DTA. Growth-phase AUM may be higher because leverage is higher; the app then computes the asset/debt reduction required to finish at the terminal DTA.

Core equations: ROE = (asset return − DTA × borrowing cost) ÷ (1 − DTA). Stressed cash-interest coverage = stressed distribution yield ÷ (DTA × stressed borrowing rate). Drawdown-to-forced-boundary = 1 − growth DTA ÷ forced-action DTA. Monthly contribution = entered new capital × deployment velocity. This is a deterministic planning model, not a forecast, investment recommendation, tax model, or lender-maintenance model.
