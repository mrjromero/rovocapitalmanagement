Simulation Summary & Chart Guide

Understanding the Data Curves

The interactive charting component maps out your asset structure over time. Here is what each line measures:

  • Total Assets (Solid Blue Area): The gross market valuation of your total holding power. It visually expands away from your net worth line based on how much structural debt you have introduced onto the balance sheet.
  • Base Equity / Leveraged (Solid Green Line): Your true personal net worth over time under this leveraged framework. It represents gross assets minus the outstanding margin debt balance. All positive carry, price appreciation, and savings injections compound directly into this curve.
  • Standard Buy-and-Hold (Dashed Amber Line): Your baseline financial control run. It outlines what your exact net worth trajectory would look like if you avoided borrowing entirely and simply bought and held the underlying assets with 100% cash, reinvesting dividends over time.
  • Inflation Breakeven Hurdle (Solid Magenta Line): The purchasing power baseline. This tracks how much your out-of-pocket savings injections must grow *just to protect your baseline purchasing power* against inflation. If your equity curve is above this line, you have real gains. If it flies drastically above it, leverage has achieved financial escape velocity.
  • Liquidation Threshold / Margin Call Line (Dotted Red Line): The critical safety floor. Calculated dynamically as Outstanding Debt ÷ (1 - Maintenance Requirement). If the blue asset area ever breaches or touches this line, your capital faces an immediate forced broker liquidation.
