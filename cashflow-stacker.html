The "When to Increase Stack Size" settings act as the throttle for the Cash Flow Stacking strategy. They dictate exactly when and how aggressively you scale up your investments as your cash flow grows.

1. Payoff Threshold (Performance-Based)

  • Tracks how many months it takes for your combined income to drive the LOC debt back to zero. If you clear the debt in less than or equal to your defined threshold, it applies the multiplier to the next purchase.

2. Every Certain Number of Stacks (Volume-Based)

  • A step-ladder approach based purely on the number of investment cycles you complete, regardless of how long they take.

3. Every Certain Number of Months (Time-Based)

  • Tied strictly to the simulation's calendar. It ignores how many stacks you've bought or how fast you are paying them off.

The "Use Credit Balance for Larger Stacks" Mechanic

Ensures you never have "lazy money" sitting idle. If you have a positive cash surplus when buying a stack:

  • If Unchecked: Deploys the exact calculated size, leaving cash uninvested.
  • If Checked: Sweeps your positive cash balance and adds it to the stack size, effectively purchasing a larger asset without taking on deeper debt.
