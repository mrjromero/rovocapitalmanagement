LowerUpperStatusInterpretation

≥ 35%< 50%GoodWell-structured portfolio: Maintenance levels are still efficient, but review new additions for higher efficiency alternatives.

≥ 65%< 80%Low EfficiencyPortfolio drag: Too many high-maintenance securities — consider replacing with margin-friendly ETFs to regain leverage flexibility.
≥ 80%< 100%Inefficient PortfolioExtremely inefficient structure: Portfolio not suitable for leverage. Replace high-maintenance holdings (e.g., individual stocks, CEFs, or REITs) with more margin-efficient securities.
