What is this tool?
This calculator bridges the gap between your portfolio's underlying Macro Leverage (Debt-to-Asset / DTA Ratio) and your M1 screen's visible Capacity Metric (Margin Utilization Rate).

Why does M1 have two different modes?
The platform calculates your borrow boundaries using two distinct risk frameworks:

• Withdrawal Basis (Cash Extraction Framework): Use this when taking physical cash out of the account or modeling automated Smart Transfer Rules. Because cash leaves the shell entirely, M1 enforces a strict, rigid credit ceiling.

• Reinvestment Basis (Collateralized Buying Power): Use this only when using margin to buy more stocks inside M1. The platform extends an augmented credit line because the newly bought shares stay inside the account as extra backup collateral.

3. Smart Transfer Guide & Guardrails

M1 Smart Transfers are strictly triggered by cash balance thresholds (fixed dollar boundaries) rather than percentage parameters. However, when an automation rule executes a sweep from your credit line, it is governed by specific structural guardrails:

  • Withdrawal Limit Dependency (Cash Extraction): Automated rules treat sweeps as capital extractions. Because the cash leaves the portfolio shell, the maximum transfer size is strictly capped by your Available for Withdrawal credit limit.
  • Reinvestment Rules Ignored: The expanded internal buying power ("Available to Invest") is completely bypassed by Smart Transfers since no balancing stock collateral is added to the account.
  • How to Calibrate: Keep the toggle set to Withdrawal Basis. Input different target cash sweep amounts as simulated debt into Card 2 to see exactly what your dashboard Margin Utilization Rate and macro DTA will look like post-transfer, ensuring your automated rules never cross critical risk limits.
