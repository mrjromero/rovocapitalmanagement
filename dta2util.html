Smart Transfer Rules Integration

M1 Smart Transfers are strictly triggered by cash balance thresholds (dollar targets) rather than percentage parameters. However, when an automation rule executes a sweep from your line of credit, it is governed by specific structural guardrails:

  • Withdrawal Limit Dependency: Automated rules treat sweeps as cash extractions. Because the capital leaves the portfolio shell, the transfer size is explicitly constrained by your Available for Withdrawal credit limit.
  • Reinvestment Rules Ignored: The expanded "Available to Invest" buying power is completely bypassed by Smart Transfers since no new balancing asset collateral is added.
  • How to Calibrate: Keep the toggle set to Withdrawal Basis. Input different target cash sweep amounts as simulated debt into the converter to determine exactly what your dashboard Margin Utilization Rate and macro DTA will look like post-transfer, helping you ensure your automated rules never cross critical risk boundaries.
