Technical Reference Manual: Freedom Date Calculator Engine

1. Core Financial Architecture & Parameter Definitions

The application projects wealth accumulation, portfolio leverage, and net cash flow under two distinct operational paradigms: Traditional (Linear Savings & Drawdown) and Stacking (Positive Carry Capital Amplifier).

System Parameters & Inputs

  • $I$: Total Monthly Gross Income ($/mo)
  • $E_0$: Baseline Monthly Living Expenses ($/mo)
  • $E_m$: Inflated Monthly Living Expenses at Month $m$
  • $E_{target}$: Target Annualized Living Expenses ($/yr)
  • $Q_0$: Starting Portfolio Net Equity ($)
  • $i_{inf}$: Annual Inflation Rate (%)
  • $r_a$: Expected Annual Capital Appreciation Rate (%)
  • $y$: Target Annual Cash Distribution Yield Rate (%)
  • $r_m$: Annual Margin Borrowing Rate (%)
  • $L_{DTA}$: Strategic Target Debt-to-Asset Ratio ($DTA = \frac{\text{Debt}}{\text{Assets}}$)
  • $L_{max}$: Maximum Credit Line Borrowing Limit (%)
  • $M_{maint}$: Maintenance Margin Threshold (%)
  • $\sigma_{ann}$: Annualized Price Volatility / Standard Deviation (%)

2. Time-Step & Rates Conversion Engine

All annual rates are converted to monthly discrete steps within the simulation engine:

$$\begin{aligned} r_{inf, m} &= (1 + i_{inf})^{1/12} - 1 \quad \text{(Compounded monthly inflation rate)} \\ \mu_{app, m} &= \frac{r_a}{12} \quad \text{(Monthly mean capital appreciation rate)} \\ y_m &= \frac{y}{12} \quad \text{(Monthly distribution yield rate)} \\ r_{margin, m} &= \frac{r_m}{12} \quad \text{(Monthly margin interest rate)} \\ \sigma_m &= \frac{\sigma_{ann}}{\sqrt{12}} \quad \text{(Monthly volatility standard deviation)} \end{aligned}$$

Stochastic Appreciation Factor

When volatility is enabled ($\sigma_{ann} > 0$), price appreciation for month $m$ and simulation run $s$ is drawn via Box-Muller transformation:

$$R_{app, m, s} = \mu_{app, m} + \left( Z_{m, s} \cdot \sigma_m \right) \quad \text{where } Z_{m,s} \sim \mathcal{N}(0,1)$$

When volatility is toggled off ($\sigma_{ann} = 0$), $R_{app, m, s} = \mu_{app, m}$.

3. Freedom Target Calculations

The Freedom Target represents the required liquid net equity ($Q_{target}$) needed to produce sufficient net income to cover living expenses without selling assets.

Mode Required Net Equity Formula
Traditional Mode $$Q_{target} = \frac{E_{target}}{y}$$
Stacking Mode (Positive Carry) $$Q_{target} = \frac{E_{target} \cdot (1 - L_{DTA})}{y_{net\_eff}}$$

Traditional Mode Target

Traditional mode assumes an unleveraged portfolio ($L_{DTA} = 0$). Net portfolio yield equals the cash distribution rate $y$:

$$Q_{target, trad}(m) = \frac{E_{target}(m)}{y} = \frac{E_m \cdot 12}{y}$$

Stacking Mode Target

Stacking utilizes strategic leverage ($L_{DTA} > 0$) to control a larger gross asset base ($A$). The effective net yield ($y_{net\_eff}$) accounts for the cost of margin debt:

$$y_{net\_eff} = \max\left(0.001, \, y - (L_{DTA} \cdot r_m)\right)$$ $$Q_{target, stack}(m) = \frac{E_{target}(m) \cdot (1 - L_{DTA})}{y_{net\_eff}} = \frac{(E_m \cdot 12) \cdot (1 - L_{DTA})}{y - (L_{DTA} \cdot r_m)}$$

4. Monthly Iteration Engines

Mode 1: Traditional Engine (Unleveraged Grind)

Traditional mode pools all external cash leftovers into un-levered liquid equity. If expenses exceed income, capital is withdrawn from net equity.

For each month $m$ and simulation iteration $s$:

  1. Calculate Inflated Expenses: $$E_m = E_0 \cdot (1 + r_{inf, m})^m$$
  2. Calculate Net Monthly Cash Surplus/Deficit: $$\Delta C_m = I - E_m$$
  3. Update Net Portfolio Equity ($Q_{m, s}$): $$Q_{m, s} = \max\left(0, \; Q_{m-1, s} + \left(Q_{m-1, s} \cdot R_{app, m, s}\right) + \left(Q_{m-1, s} \cdot y_m\right) + \Delta C_m\right)$$

Mode 2: Stacking Engine (Positive Carry Amplifier)

Stacking models gross assets ($A$), margin debt ($D$), and net equity ($Q = A - D$). High-yield cash flow and active income are 100% swept against debt, while margin absorbs any expense deficits.

Stacking State Pipeline Flow:
1. [Initial Equity Q] → [Gross Assets A = Q / (1 - L_DTA)] → [Margin Debt D = A - Q]
2. [Monthly Asset Evolution] → Apply $R_{app}$ to $A$
3. [Debt Sweep] → Update $D$ with distributions, active income, expenses, and interest charges
4. [Rebalancing Engine] → Recouple leverage back to target $L_{DTA}$ if required

For each month $m$ and simulation iteration $s$:

  1. Asset Price Evolution: $$A_{curr} = A_{m-1, s} \cdot (1 + R_{app, m, s})$$
  2. Income & Cost Sweeps on Debt Balance:
    $$\begin{aligned} \text{Distribution Yield Income} &= A_{m-1, s} \cdot y_m \\ \text{Margin Interest Charge} &= D_{m-1, s} \cdot r_{margin, m} \\ \text{Float Juice Adjustment} &= I \cdot (\mu_{app, m} + y_m) \end{aligned}$$ $$\Delta D_m = E_m + \text{Margin Interest Charge} - I - \text{Distribution Yield Income} - \text{Float Juice Adjustment}$$ $$D_{curr} = D_{m-1, s} + \Delta D_m$$
  3. Calculate Unadjusted Net Equity: $$Q_{curr} = A_{curr} - D_{curr}$$
  4. Equity Rebalancing & Leveraged Recoupling:
    If $Q_{curr} \le 0$, the portfolio suffers total liquidation ($A_{m, s} = 0$, $D_{m, s} = 0$, $Q_{m, s} = 0$).

    If $Q_{curr} > 0$, compare actual leverage ($L_{curr} = \frac{D_{curr}}{A_{curr}}$) to $L_{DTA}$:
    $$\text{If } L_{curr} < L_{DTA}: \quad \begin{cases} A_{target} = \dfrac{Q_{curr}}{1 - L_{DTA}} \\[8pt] \Delta D_{borrow} = A_{target} - A_{curr} \\[4pt] A_{m, s} = A_{curr} + \Delta D_{borrow} \\[4pt] D_{m, s} = D_{curr} + \Delta D_{borrow} \end{cases}$$ $$\text{If } L_{curr} \ge L_{DTA}: \quad \begin{cases} A_{m, s} = A_{curr} \\[4pt] D_{m, s} = D_{curr} \end{cases}$$
  5. Final Net Equity: $$Q_{m, s} = A_{m, s} - D_{m, s}$$

5. Risk Guardrails & Liquidity Metrics

Credit Line Utilization

Given maximum allowable leverage factor $L_{max}$:

$$\begin{aligned} \text{Total Credit Line} &= A \cdot L_{max} \\ \text{Credit Utilization (\%)} &= \begin{cases} \left(\frac{D}{A \cdot L_{max}}\right) \cdot 100 & \text{if } A > 0 \\ 0 & \text{if } A = 0 \end{cases} \end{aligned}$$

Downside Drop to Margin Call

Given maintenance margin requirement $M_{maint}$ (minimum equity percentage $\frac{Q}{A} \ge M_{maint}$):

$$\begin{aligned} A_{call} &= \frac{D}{1 - M_{maint}} \quad \text{(Asset value triggering a margin call)} \\ \text{Drop to Margin Call (\%)} &= \begin{cases} \left(\frac{A - A_{call}}{A}\right) \cdot 100 & \text{if } D > 0 \text{ and } M_{maint} < 1 \\ 100\% & \text{if } D = 0 \end{cases} \end{aligned}$$

6. Optimization Engine (optimizeArchitecture)

Executes a bounded grid search over parameter spaces to identify configurations yielding the fastest time-to-freedom ($m_{freedom}$) while satisfying safety guardrails.

Bounded Search Domain

  • $L_{DTA} \in [15\%, 35\%]$, step $= 2.5\%$
  • $y \in [8\%, 15\%]$, step $= 0.5\%$

Evaluation Constraints & Guardrail Rules

  1. Positive Carry Constraint: Annual spread must be strictly positive: $$(A_0 \cdot y) - (D_0 \cdot r_m) > 0 \quad \text{where } A_0 = \frac{Q_0}{1 - L_{DTA}}, \; D_0 = A_0 - Q_0$$
  2. Credit Utilization Guardrail: $\text{Utilization} \le 70\%$
  3. Margin Call Cushion Guardrail: $\text{Drop to Margin Call} \ge 25\%$
  4. Monthly Net Cash Flow Constraint: $$\left(I - E_0\right) + \frac{(A_0 \cdot y) - (D_0 \cdot r_m)}{12} > 0$$

Objective Function

Minimize estimated months to target ($m_{est}$):

$$m_{est} = \frac{Q_{target, stack} - Q_0}{(I - E_0) + \frac{(A_0 \cdot y) - (D_0 \cdot r_m)}{12}}$$

Capital appreciation ($r_a$) scales dynamically based on yield aggression:

$$r_a = \begin{cases} 2.5\% & \text{if } y \ge 12\% \\ 3.5\% & \text{if } 10\% \le y < 12\% \\ 4.5\% & \text{if } y < 10\% \end{cases}$$

7. Stochastic Monte Carlo Engine

  • Sample Size: $N = 500$ trajectories.
  • Duration: $M = 360$ months (30 years).
  • Percentile Aggregations: Array values at month $m$ across all $N$ runs are sorted ascendingly to derive distribution boundaries:
    • $P_{10}$ (Conservative): Value at index $\lfloor 0.10 \times N \rfloor$
    • $P_{50}$ (Expected / Median):** Value at index $\lfloor 0.50 \times N \rfloor$
    • $P_{90}$ (Optimistic): Value at index $\lfloor 0.90 \times N \rfloor$
