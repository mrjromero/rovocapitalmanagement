Stratified Framework Summary

  • Strategy 1: Policy-First (Standard Flow): Routes 100% of income through the policy monthly. High safety, but capital faces heavy timing bottlenecks inside the internal carrier holding accounts.
  • Strategy 2: Portfolio-First (Single Engine): Bypasses insurance up front to maximize raw equity exposure. Erases early fee load friction, but traps capital single-filed and eliminates secondary tier collateral structures.
  • Strategy 3: Hybrid Margin Bridge (Max Velocity): Deploys a temporary short-term margin bridge on Day 1 to fully exhaust non-MEC room instantly. Policy loans retire the bridge, allowing ongoing monthly paychecks to feed the portfolio entirely unhindered. Result: Captures the highest amount of contractually guaranteed, uncallable collateral.

System Architecture Glossary

  • Debt-to-Asset (DTA) Ratio: Total margin liabilities divided by gross brokerage account holdings. Keeping this parameter compressed protects the wealth machine from flash margin calls.
  • Interim Allocation Tank: The transient parking bucket inside an IUL where mid-year cash additions linger at flat rates while awaiting the official anniversary window.
  • Participating Policy Loan: A structured contract loan where your cash value collateral is maintained inside the index options pool, maximizing multi-asset parallel compounding.
