Model boundaries

Important assumptions and limitations
  • Economic return and borrowing rates are constant; actual returns, rates, collateral values, and lender requirements change.
  • Cash distribution yield is used only for interest-coverage analysis. It is not added on top of economic total return.
  • Annualized volatility is descriptive. The entered design drawdown—not volatility alone—determines the modeled leverage limit.
  • The stress test assumes debt is unchanged during an immediate asset-price decline and calculates the resulting LTV before corrective action.
  • Acquisition mode assumes monthly rebalancing and reinvestment to target LTV. Buy-borrow-die mode assumes incremental borrowing or repayment to target LTV and reports net cash extracted.
  • The model excludes taxes, fees, slippage, distribution changes, NAV erosion not captured in economic return, liquidity constraints, sequence risk, and forced-sale costs.
  • This is an educational illustration, not financial, lending, tax, legal, or estate-planning advice.
