📐 YieldCanary Mathematical & Technical Manual

A mathematical specification of all analytical formulas, boundary behaviors, resilience scoring algorithms, and gatekeeper optimization functions.

1. Primary Variables & Notational Definitions

Variable Dimension / Unit Formal Description
$P_{\text{current}}$ USD (\$) Current market closing share price of the target fund at evaluation date $t_0$.
$P_{1\text{Y ago}}$ USD (\$) Historical market closing share price exactly 1 year (365 calendar days) prior to $t_0$.
$\sum D_{12\text{M}}$ USD (\$) Sum of all cash distributions declared and paid per share over the preceding 12 months.
$D_{\text{latest}}$ USD (\$) Dollar value of the single most recent per-share distribution payment.
$D_{\text{last month}}$ USD (\$) Total per-share distributions paid within the most recent full calendar month.
$N$ Integer Annual distribution frequency: Weekly ($N=52$), Monthly ($N=12$), or Quarterly ($N=4$).
$t$ Decimal Marginal tax rate applicable to distributions (default: $t = 0.20$).
$\text{ROC}_{\text{dest}}$ Decimal $[0, 1]$ Proportion of distributions classified as destructive Return of Capital (unearned principal).
$r_{\text{debt}}$ Decimal / % Baseline cost of leverage / debt hurdle rate ($r_{\text{debt}} = 4.15\%$).

2. Yield & Cash Flow Formulas

A. Headline Yield (Trailing 12-Month / TTM)

Measures the backward-looking cash flow delivered relative to current purchasing price.

Equation 1: Headline Yield
$$\text{Headline Yield} = \left( \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{k} D_i}{P_{\text{current}}} \right) \times 100$$

B. Advertised Yield (Forward Annualized Run-Rate)

Projects the annualized forward distribution rate quoted by fund sponsors.

Equation 2: Advertised Yield
$$\text{Advertised Yield} = \left( \frac{D_{\text{latest}} \times N}{P_{\text{current}}} \right) \times 100$$

C. True Income Yield & ROC Factorization

Isolates real economic income from capital liquidation by filtering out unearned Return of Capital.

Equation 3: True Income Yield
$$\text{True Income Yield} = \begin{cases} \text{Headline Yield}, & \text{if fund holds } \text{🏅 Tax-Efficient ROC Badge} \\ \text{Headline Yield} \times (1 - \text{ROC}_{\text{dest}}), & \text{for standard / destructive ROC funds} \end{cases}$$
Mathematical Justification: For Section 1256 index option overlay funds (e.g., SPYI, QQQI), accounting ROC represents tax-deferral mechanics without NAV erosion. For single-stock or capped-upside funds (e.g., TSLY, NVDY), destructive ROC directly cannibalizes the share base.

3. Total Net Return & After-Tax Performance Formulas

A. 1-Year Price Return ($\Delta P_{1\text{Y}}$)

Measures pure capital preservation or decay over a 12-month trailing horizon.

Equation 4: 1-Year Price Return
$$\text{Price Return}_{1\text{Y}} = \left( \frac{P_{\text{current}} - P_{1\text{Y ago}}}{P_{1\text{Y ago}}} \right) \times 100$$

B. Monthly Spendable Cash Yield ($Y_{\text{spendable}}$)

Computes the actual monthly cash in pocket delivered after paying income taxes.

Equation 5: Monthly Spendable Cash Yield
$$Y_{\text{spendable}} = \left( \frac{D_{\text{last month}} \times (1 - t)}{P_{\text{current}}} \right) \times 100$$

C. Take-Home Cash Return ($R_{\text{net}}$ / Net Total Return)

The core wealth-expansion metric. Combines pure share price return with total after-tax cash distributions relative to the initial 1-year investment base.

Equation 6: Take-Home Cash Return
$$R_{\text{net}} = \text{Price Return}_{1\text{Y}} + \left( \frac{\sum D_{12\text{M}} \times (1 - t)}{P_{1\text{Y ago}}} \right) \times 100$$

4. Capital Longevity & Death Clock Dynamics

A. Annualized Destructive Decay Velocity ($\delta_{\text{NAV}}$)

When a fund experiences negative price return driven by unearned distributions, its decay velocity is:

Equation 7: Decay Rate
$$\delta_{\text{NAV}} = \max\left(0, \; -\frac{\text{Price Return}_{1\text{Y}}}{100}\right)$$

B. Death Clock ($T_{\text{death}}$ / NAV Halving Horizon)

Calculates the exact projected time in years required for the asset's principal base to decline by 50% ($0.50$):

Equation 8: Death Clock (Years)
$$T_{\text{death}} = \begin{cases} \text{N/A}, & \text{if } \text{Price Return}_{1\text{Y}} \ge 0 \lor \text{Badge} \\ \frac{0.50}{\delta_{\text{NAV}}}, & \text{if } \text{Price Return}_{1\text{Y}} < 0 \end{cases}$$
Strategic Significance: Because our positive-carry debt arbitrage is modeled as a 36-month amortizing note ($3.0 \text{ Years}$), any fund with $T_{\text{death}} \le 3.0 \text{ Years}$ will halve before returning debt capital, triggering automatic gatekeeper rejection.

5. Payout Resilience Grading Model (100-Point Function)

The total resilience score $S_{\text{total}}$ is a composite piecewise function of 4 orthogonal pillars:

Equation 9: Composite Resilience Score
$$S_{\text{total}} = S_1(R_{\text{net}}) + S_2(\text{Price Return}_{1\text{Y}}) + S_3(\text{Coverage}) + S_4(T_{\text{death}}) \quad \in [0, 100]$$

Scoring Sub-Functions ($S_1, S_2, S_3, S_4$)

Pillar Weight Piecewise Threshold Function
$S_1$: Take-Home Return 35 Pts $$S_1 = \begin{cases} 35, & R_{\text{net}} > 15\% \\ 28, & 10\% < R_{\text{net}} \le 15\% \\ 20, & 5\% < R_{\text{net}} \le 10\% \\ 10, & 0\% \le R_{\text{net}} \le 5\% \\ 0, & R_{\text{net}} < 0\% \end{cases}$$
$S_2$: NAV Preservation 30 Pts $$S_2 = \begin{cases} 30, & \Delta P > 5\% \\ 25, & 0\% \le \Delta P \le 5\% \\ 15, & -5\% \le \Delta P < 0\% \\ 5, & -15\% \le \Delta P < -5\% \\ 0, & \Delta P < -15\% \end{cases}$$
$S_3$: Earned Coverage 20 Pts $$S_3 = \begin{cases} 20, & \text{Coverage} = 100\% \lor \text{Badge} \\ 15, & 85\% \le \text{Coverage} < 100\% \\ 10, & 70\% \le \text{Coverage} < 85\% \\ 0, & \text{Coverage} < 70\% \end{cases}$$
$S_4$: Death Clock 15 Pts $$S_4 = \begin{cases} 15, & T_{\text{death}} = \text{N/A} \\ 12, & T_{\text{death}} > 10.0 \text{ Yrs} \\ 8, & 5.0 \le T_{\text{death}} \le 10.0 \text{ Yrs} \\ 0, & T_{\text{death}} < 5.0 \text{ Yrs} \end{cases}$$

6. The 36-Month Note Gatekeeper Decision Function

The gatekeeper decision is evaluated through a strict ternary conjunction (logical AND) optimization rule:

Equation 11: Binary Gatekeeper Filter
$$\text{Gatekeeper}(X) = \begin{cases} \mathbf{\text{✅ APPROVED: 36-Mo Note Equivalent}}, & \text{if } \Big( \text{True Income Yield} > 4.15\% \Big) \land \Big( T_{\text{death}} > 3.0 \lor T_{\text{death}} = \text{N/A} \Big) \land \Big( S_{\text{total}} \ge 60 \Big) \\ \mathbf{\text{❌ REJECTED: Terminal NAV Decay}}, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
Positive Carry Spread ($\Delta_{\text{carry}}$): $$\Delta_{\text{carry}} = \text{True Income Yield} - r_{\text{debt}} = \text{True Income Yield} - 4.15\% > 0$$
Amortization Window Constraint: $$T_{\text{death}} > T_{\text{amort}} = 3.0 \text{ Years}$$
