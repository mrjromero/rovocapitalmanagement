🐤 YieldCanary Metric Guide & User Manual

A structured guide to the metrics, calculations, resilience grading, and gatekeeper logic in the /YieldCanary financial engine.

1. The 36-Month Amortizing Note Architecture

YieldCanary evaluates derivative income ETFs, option strategies, and closed-end funds (CEFs) not as permanent equity positions, but as 36-month amortizing notes designed to capture a positive carry spread against a 4.15% cost of debt.

The Strategy Objective:

Extract 12% to 40%+ cash flows to pay down debt and recover principal within a 3-year timeline.

The Threat (NAV Decay):

Many option-income funds sell upside while bearing full downside risk, eroding their underlying Net Asset Value (NAV).

The Gatekeeper:

YieldCanary strips away promotional headline yields to verify that actual fund earnings survive past 36 months.

2. Core Yield & Income Metrics

A. Headline Yield (TTM)

The backward-looking trailing 12-month distribution yield delivered per share relative to the current share price.

Headline Yield (%) = (Sum of 12M Payouts / Current Share Price) * 100

B. Advertised Yield (Forward Rate)

The forward-projected annual distribution rate quoted by fund managers, annualizing the most recent payout.

Advertised Yield (%) = (Latest Payout * Payout Frequency [12 or 52] / Current Share Price) * 100

C. Return of Capital (ROC %) & True Income Yield

YieldCanary classifies Return of Capital into two categories based on underlying strategy mechanics:

🏅 Constructive ROC (Tax-Efficient Badge):

Applies to funds (e.g., SPYI, QQQI) using Section 1256 index option spreads that generate real economic income but classify payouts as ROC to defer taxes for investors without NAV decay.

True Income Yield = Headline Yield
Destructive ROC (Principal Erosion):

Applies when payouts exceed fund total returns, forcing the fund to liquidate principal and causing structural NAV loss.

True Income Yield = Headline Yield * (1 - Destructive ROC %)

3. Total Return & Cash Flow Metrics

A. 1-Year Price Return

Measures pure capital preservation over the preceding 12 months, excluding distributions.

1Y Price Return (%) = ((Current Price - Price 1Y Ago) / Price 1Y Ago) * 100

B. Monthly Spendable Cash Yield

The net after-tax spendable cash generated per dollar invested ($t = 20\%$).

Monthly Spendable Yield (%) = (Last Month Payout * (1 - 0.20) / Current Price) * 100

C. Take-Home Cash Return (Net Total Return)

The true 1-year net investor return, combining capital price change and after-tax distributions.

Take-Home Cash Return (%) = 1Y Price Return + ((Sum of 12M Payouts * (1 - 0.20)) / Price 1Y Ago) * 100

4. Risk & Longevity Metrics

A. Death Clock (NAV Halving Horizon)

The projected years required for share price/NAV to decline by 50% based on annual capital decay. Returns N/A if NAV is stable/growing or if the Tax-Efficient Badge applies.

Death Clock (Years) = 0.50 / Annualized NAV Decay Rate

B. Canary Health Tiers

Status Health Tier Operational Meaning
🟢 Healthy Fully earned income from dividends, interest, or trading gains with stable NAV.
🏅 Tax-Efficient Badge Constructive Section 1256 option deferral; NAV preserved.
🟡 Watch Moderate unearned ROC or slight NAV drag; requires regular monitoring.
🟠 High Risk Noticeable destructive ROC; share price in clear downward trend (-10% to -15% 1Y).
🔴 Severe Risk Terminal NAV decay (<-15% 1Y); rapid principal liquidation.

5. 100-Point Payout Resilience Scoring Model

A quantitative framework evaluating whether a fund expands wealth or destroys principal:

Pillar Max Points Scoring Tiers
1. Take-Home Cash Return 35 Pts >+15% (35 pts) | +10% to +15% (28 pts) | +5% to +10% (20 pts) | 0% to +5% (10 pts) | <0% (0 pts)
2. NAV Preservation 30 Pts >+5% (30 pts) | 0% to +5% (25 pts) | -5% to 0% (15 pts) | -15% to -5% (5 pts) | <-15% (0 pts)
3. Earned Coverage Ratio 20 Pts 100% Coverage / Badge (20 pts) | 85%-99% (15 pts) | 70%-84% (10 pts) | <70% (0 pts)
4. Death Clock Longevity 15 Pts N/A (15 pts) | >10 Years (12 pts) | 5 to 10 Years (8 pts) | <5 Years (0 pts)
Grade A+ / A (90–100 Pts): Superior resilience; NAV expands alongside payouts.
Grade B+ / B (75–89 Pts): Strong resilience; solid total return with minor drag.
Grade C+ / C (60–74 Pts): Moderate resilience; positive return with noticeable NAV drag.
Grade D / F (<60 Pts): Severe capital erosion; high yields offset by continuous decay.

6. The 36-Month Amortizing Note Gatekeeper Rule

✅ APPROVED: 36-Mo Note Equivalent

Required Conditions:

  • True Income Yield > 4.15% (Positive Carry Spread)
  • Death Clock > 3.0 Years or N/A (Survives 36-Month Window)
  • Resilience Grade ≥ C (Score ≥ 60 Pts)

❌ REJECTED: Terminal NAV Decay

Failure Triggers:

  • Death Clock ≤ 3.0 Years (Decays before principal recovery)
  • True Income Yield ≤ 4.15% (Fails debt hurdle)
  • Resilience Grade D or F (Capital destruction)
